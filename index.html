Biography

I am a senior algorithm researcher at Alibaba DAMO Academy, and am also a postdoc researcher at Zhejiang University. Before that, I was a research fellow at the Australia Institute for Machine Learning (AIML), University of Adelaide (UoA), Australia, jointly work with Dr. Johan Verjans. Before that, I was a visitor at UoA under the supervision of Prof. Chunhua Shen.

My research interests span a broad spectrum in the medical AI domain, including but not limited to multi-modal learning, large langauge model, and vision-language pre-training. The core objective of my work is to harness the potential of AI technology in clinical environments, thereby enhancing its value through direct applications that benefit healthcare professionals and patients alike.

We are recruiting interns with a passion and research background in multi-modal large language models. If you are interested, please send me your resume.
