Biography

I am currently a Staff Algorithm Engineer at Alibaba DAMO Academy. I previously worked as a postdoctoral researcher at Zhejiang University. Prior to that, I was a Research Fellow at the Australian Institute for Machine Learning (AIML), University of Adelaide (UoA), where I collaborated with Dr. Johan Verjans. Before joining AIML, I was a visiting researcher at UoA under the supervision of Prof. Chunhua Shen.

My research interests span a broad spectrum in AI for healthcare, including but not limited to multi-modal learning, large langauge model, and vision-language pre-training. The core objective of my work is to harness the potential of AI technology in clinical environments, thereby enhancing its value through direct applications that benefit healthcare professionals and patients alike.

We are recruiting interns with a passion and research background in multi-modal large language models. If you are interested, please send me your resume.
